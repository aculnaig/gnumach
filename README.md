Unofficial mirror of GNU Mach at https://cgit.git.savannah.gnu.org/cgit/hurd/gnumach.git/

This is a complete rewrite effort to port GNU Mach to ARM architecture.

This is a heavy experimental project. DO NOT use on bare-metal.

This mirror will be updated on a weekly basis, or may be not. Depends on the mood and free time over the weekend.
